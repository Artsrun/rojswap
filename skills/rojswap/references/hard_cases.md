# Hard-case notes (skill reference)

Sunglasses + strong red lighting is the canonical hard case.

- Pixel-level swaps leave sunglasses or soft eye regions.
- Diffusion models can generate plausible eyes and match lighting but may drift body or fine motion.
- Always gate on a 5–10 s short clip.
- Prefer Wan 2.2 Animate Replace (or cloud equivalent) as the first diffusion runner.
